Help Center

Support

Learn Japanese for Beginners is an offline course for first steps in Japanese: hiragana, katakana, beginner kanji, vocabulary, phrases, and quizzes.

Supported platforms

Confirmed in the application project: iPhone, iPad, and an Apple Watch companion (scheme LingoWatch). An Android build exists in the same Flutter project; this Support page is written for Apple App Store users.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes for lessons, quizzes, favorites, and progress. Those materials are stored on the device. Pronunciation needs a Japanese (ja-JP) voice already installed on the device.
I hear no Japanese on a card.
This version uses on-device speech, not bundled studio lesson files. On iPhone or iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then download a Japanese voice (Kyoko is preferred when available). Also check the app’s sound switch and the device Silent / Ring switch and volume.
Interface sounds play, but Japanese does not.
Tap sounds are bundled in the app. Lesson pronunciation is operating-system text-to-speech of Japanese script. Install a Japanese voice as above. English or romaji is not spoken.
Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?
Not in this version. There is no paywall and no Restore Purchases control.
Apple Watch is empty.
Open the iPhone app, choose words or phrases in the Apple Watch area, then sync. The Watch shows items you send. It does not download a separate catalog from the internet.
I want another interface language.
Settings → Translation language. You still study Japanese. The interface language only changes chrome and meanings.
Where is my progress stored?
On this device only (favorites, learned items, quiz misses, language and sound preferences). Deleting the app removes it. There is no cloud backup from us.
The app will not install or update.
Confirm you are signed into the App Store, have storage space, and a supported iOS / iPadOS version for the build you downloaded. If a Watch app is missing, pair the Watch and install the companion from the Watch app on iPhone.

How to request technical support

Open a GitHub Issue

GitHub sign-in is required to submit an issue. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you expected to hear or see. Do not paste Apple ID passwords, certificates, or other secrets.
