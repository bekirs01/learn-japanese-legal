Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026. This policy describes the Learn Japanese for Beginners app as inspected in source. It is not legal advice.

1. Application and developer

This policy applies to Learn Japanese for Beginners (home-screen name Learn Japanese), bundle identifier com.bekirsucikaran.learnjapanese, including the Apple Watch companion com.bekirsucikaran.learnjapanese.watchkitapp.

The app is an offline beginner Japanese course for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. The operator of this website is the app’s developer, Bekir Sucikaran. There is no documented public support email in the application source. Privacy questions can be sent through GitHub Issues on this public legal repository (a GitHub account is required to open an issue).

2. Summary

The app does not require an account. Core lessons, quizzes, favorites, and progress work without the internet. This version does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social login, Firebase, Supabase, or a developer-operated backend. Learning data is stored on your device, not on our servers — this app has none.

Apple’s privacy manifests shipped with the iPhone and Watch apps declare that the app does not track users, lists no collected data types for App Privacy reporting, and declares UserDefaults access with reason CA92.1 (app functionality).

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores a small set of preferences in on-device key-value storage (Apple UserDefaults via the Flutter shared_preferences plugin). This information is used only to run the app on that device:

  • Interface / translation language
  • Favorite word and phrase identifiers
  • Learned word and phrase identifiers
  • Quiz misses used for wrong-answer review
  • Sound-effect and haptics on/off switches
  • Apple Watch learning draft (items you chose to send to the Watch)

Uninstalling the iPhone app (and the Watch app, if installed) removes this storage. You can also clear favorites, learned items, and quiz misses inside the app.

4. Audio and pronunciation

Speaker controls speak Japanese script using the operating system’s text-to-speech engine (ja-JP, Kyoko when that voice is installed) through the flutter_tts plugin. This version ships no bundled studio or educational speech recordings for lessons. Interface tap sounds are short files bundled in the app and played locally with audioplayers.

The app does not record your voice, does not use the microphone, and does not use speech recognition. We do not ship a cloud speech API key. When a Japanese voice is installed, synthesis is expected to stay on the device. We cannot promise that every operating-system TTS implementation never contacts its vendor; that behavior belongs to Apple or Google, not to a server of ours.

5. Apple Watch

If you use the companion Watch app, the iPhone can send words and phrases you select to your paired Watch using Apple Watch Connectivity. That transfer stays between your devices. The Watch may keep the latest items in on-device storage so they remain available when the iPhone is not nearby. The Watch does not download a separate cloud catalog.

6. Network communication

Lessons, quizzes, and progress do not require the internet. The Android release manifest in source does not declare INTERNET permission. Debug or profile Android builds used by Flutter tooling may include network permission for development; that is not required for the lesson catalog.

7. Purchases

This version does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or StoreKit products. Apple’s App Store may still process the free download of the app. We do not receive card numbers or Apple ID passwords.

8. Analytics, tracking, and advertising

This binary does not include an analytics SDK, crash reporter, advertising SDK, or tracking across other companies’ apps. It does not use App Tracking Transparency because it does not track.

9. Third-party software in the app

The app is built with Flutter. Production Dart dependencies used for user-facing behavior are:

  • shared_preferences — on-device preferences
  • audioplayers — local playback of bundled interface sounds
  • flutter_tts — operating-system Japanese speech
  • characters — Unicode text handling

These plugins are not used here to send your learning data to their publishers. Apple provides iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, Watch Connectivity, UserDefaults, and speech synthesis as operating-system features.

10. Data we do not collect

We do not collect name, email, phone number, address, payment details, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone recordings, browsing history, advertising identifiers, cookies, or diagnostics sent to us. We do not operate an account or profile server for this app.

“No Data Collected” in App Store Connect is intended to mean: this binary does not collect data off-device for the developer. Local UserDefaults still exist on the device, as described above.

11. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not receive your learning data, we do not retain it on our systems. Delete the app to remove on-device storage. Opening an issue on the public legal repository stores whatever you choose to type there, under GitHub’s terms, so that we can answer your question.

12. Children’s privacy

The course is general education. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian may install and use the app without creating a profile. Complete Apple’s age questionnaire in App Store Connect honestly when submitting the app.

13. Security

Learning state stays in the platform key-value store on your device. There is no developer login, cloud backup of that state, or application server to harden. Protect the device with the lock screen and operating-system updates you already use.

14. International users

The App Store listing targeted by this policy is English (U.S.). The app’s study language is Japanese. The interface can be set to several learner languages. Local storage remains on the device wherever you use the app. If you contact us through GitHub Issues, GitHub may process that message on its own infrastructure.

15. Privacy rights

Because we do not hold an account or a copy of your favorites on our servers, there is no cloud profile for us to export or erase. You control on-device data by using in-app controls or deleting the app. You may still ask a privacy question through the Issues link above.

16. Policy updates

If a later version adds accounts, analytics, advertising, paid unlocks, or cloud speech, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version ships. The effective date at the top of this page will change.

17. Contact

Support and privacy requests: github.com/bekirs01/learn-japanese-legal/issues. See also the Support page.
